      How Mission Mirror Works
      1. Plan Your Outpost Budget

      Allocate 100 simulation credits among 6 sectors: Power, Life Support, Water Recycling, Food Production, Radiation Shielding, and Emergency Reserves. Every credit choice involves real engineering trade-offs.

      2. Navigate Daily Operations & Cascading Physics

      Subsystems are interconnected: if your power drops below 25%, life-support scrubbers and water pumps lose efficiency, starving your crew of oxygen and triggering dehydration. Click on the Engineering Consequence Explorer to inspect connections.

      3. Dynamic Emergencies & Triage Decisions

      Face 6 unpredictable space anomalies including coronal mass ejections, filtration clogs, and pressure seal leaks. Choose between spending emergency reserves, overclocking power, or accepting crew fatigue.

      4. Counterfactual Replay & Learning

      At any point, launch a counterfactual replay of your exact mission. Make different choices to discover whether conserving spare parts or spending energy earlier would have saved the mission.
